Fare Booster – Extra amount motion · 2, 3 and 4 digits

Pop-up shows at 0 ms. After 500 ms the loop starts and repeats every 1,250 ms: pulse + shimmer + ripple for 500 ms, then a 750 ms pause.
The ring hugs the amount but is never taller than the 3-digit circle (58 px); wider amounts end as a capsule of that height.
Preview uses the system font instead of PP Mori, so sizes here are a few px larger than the spec; the cap is taken from the 3-digit reference so the behaviour matches.

Reduced motion is on for this device, so the loop is paused.
